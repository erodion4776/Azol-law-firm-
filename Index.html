Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common immigration questions

How long does the visa application process take?
Processing times vary depending on the visa type and current USCIS workload. Family-based petitions typically take 12-24 months, while employment visas may take 6-12 months. We provide realistic timelines during your consultation.
What documents do I need for my case?
Required documents vary by case type but generally include: valid passport, birth certificate, marriage certificate (if applicable), financial documents, and photos. We provide a comprehensive checklist tailored to your specific case.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we understand immigration can be expensive. We offer flexible payment plans to make our services accessible. Contact us to discuss payment options that work for your budget.
Can you help if my visa was denied?
Absolutely. We specialize in appeals and have successfully overturned many denials. We'll review your case, identify the issues that led to denial, and develop a strong strategy for your appeal.
