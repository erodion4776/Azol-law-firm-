About CYAzor Law Firm

Your Trusted Partner in U.S. Immigration & Visa Services

Our Story

Founded with a passion for helping individuals and families achieve their American dreams, CYAzor Law Firm has been serving the community with dedication and expertise, making the complex U.S. immigration process simple and accessible.

We understand that navigating visa applications and immigration procedures can be overwhelming and stressful. That's why we're committed to providing personalized, compassionate support that puts your needs first and guides you through every step of your journey.

Our service combines deep immigration knowledge with genuine care for our clients, ensuring that every case receives the attention, expertise, and personalized guidance it deserves. As U.S. immigration law is federal, we proudly represent clients in all 50 states from our Tennessee office.

Meet the Attorney

Licensed Alabama Attorney

Attorney Cynthia Azor.

Immigration Attorney

Licensed Alabama Attorney | Nationwide Immigration Representation

Attorney Cynthia Azor. is licensed to practice law in Alabama and represents immigration clients in all 50 states, as U.S. immigration law is federal and not restricted by state boundaries.

She earned her LL.B from the University of Benin, completed her B.L at the Nigerian Law School, and obtained her LL.M in American & Transnational Law from the University of Dayton School of Law, along with a Certificate in the American Legal System.

With over five years of immigration experience, she has successfully assisted clients with naturalization, green cards, family-based petitions, adjustment of status, VAWA cases, waivers, work authorization, and other immigration benefits.
